## What this is

An always-on web radio of the CODE Eternal™ ecosystem. Free to listen, no account needed.
Registered listeners can save tracks and earn $GALATIN.

## Who makes the music

AIfa™ and DJ Galatin — the project's own AI persona and the Architect, Maksim Valentinovich

tool and not a tool left alone: the songs come out of a long back-and-forth, and neither
side's part can be pulled cleanly out of the result.
